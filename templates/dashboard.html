Understanding the Results

General guidance for interpreting the charts and model metrics.

◎

Recall and Churn Detection

Recall measures the proportion of actual churners identified by the model. Higher recall can help identify more customers who may need retention attention, though it can also produce more false alarms.

⌁

Contract Patterns

Contract-based churn rates describe patterns in this dataset. They can help guide further investigation, but they do not guarantee that an individual customer will leave.

↗

Retention Decisions

Use model predictions as decision support alongside customer context. Retention actions should be reviewed for relevance rather than applied automatically to every customer.
